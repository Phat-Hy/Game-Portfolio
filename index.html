Backend & live ops

AI Balancing Service

Solo — .NET 9 · PostgreSQL · Azure

The gameplay loop alone can't tell you whether the enemies are fair. This service closes that loop: it ingests combat telemetry from the Unity client, scores each enemy configuration on a fitness function, and breeds the next generation of difficulty parameters with a genetic algorithm — independently for every player.

Four candidates per generation, elitist crossover, ±5% mutation, and an evidence gate that blocks evolution until every candidate has enough real fights. Admin dashboard for live logs and manual overrides; CI/CD through GitHub Actions to Azure App Service.

  • .NET 9
  • ASP.NET Core
  • Genetic algorithm
  • PostgreSQL
  • Azure

About

I studied Software Engineering at FPT University — an AQAS-accredited programme (major code 7480201, 148 credits) built on project-based learning, where every semester ends in a real deliverable rather than an exam.

That structure is why I ended up on the engineering side of games rather than the design side: I like the part where a system has to survive contact with players. My capstone work became a Steam-bound FPS, and the AI I wrote for it is the piece I am most proud of, because enemy behaviour is where gameplay, performance and readability all collide.

Outside of that I keep building small things to learn mechanics properly — the fastest way to understand why a game feels the way it does is to rebuild the part that makes it feel that way.
